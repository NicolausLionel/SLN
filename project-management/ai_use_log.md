## Documentation Principles

The team should follow these principles when using AI tools:

- **Verify information:** Check important claims against reliable sources.
- **Review AI-generated content:** Edit suggestions to match the project's learning objectives.
- **Maintain transparency:** Record significant AI assistance accurately.
- **Use human judgment:** Team members remain responsible for decisions and final submissions.
- **Keep records accurate:** Document actual AI use without inventing activities or results.
