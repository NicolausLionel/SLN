## Power-up log · AI use

| | |

| **Date and tool** | 6 November 2026, Claude AI |
| **Purpose** | To create a voiceover for our video tutorials |

### Important interactions
I asked Claude to “make the voice over for the video but don't change the content”. It added an AI voice-over to all 5 videos and kept the pictures, slides, speech bubbles and script exactly the same, adding only the audio track. Each member's robot got its own voice (for example 周利福 male, 曾瑞林 female, 墨仁 a lighter male voice), the narration follows the voice-over script word for word and starts when each speech bubble appears, and our names are read from simple respellings so they sound close to the real pronunciation.

### Verification and judgment
The AI said it could not listen to the audio itself, so I watched all five videos from start to end before submitting. I checked that the narration matched the script word for word, that each line started with the right speech bubble, that our names and AI terms were pronounced acceptably and that the volume was even. I noted a few lines that were read too fast and confirmed the files (23–36 MB) were under the 250 MB limit.

### Outcome and learning
All five videos now have narration, so students can follow them more easily. I learned to give the AI clear limits such as “don't change the content”, and that AI-generated audio still needs a human listener: even the AI told us to check it before submitting.
