## Learning outcomes

By the end of the module, a learner should be able to:

| # | Outcome | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| LO1 | **Explain** what Artificial Intelligence is, how it developed, and how it differs from traditional software | 1 |
| LO2 | **Describe** how data, algorithms and machine learning help AI systems produce outputs | 2 |
| LO3 | **Give examples** of where people use AI in study, work and daily life | 3 |
| LO4 | **Weigh** the benefits of AI against problems such as mistakes, hallucinations and unfair bias | 4 |
| LO5 | **Explain** the key ethical principles of privacy, fairness, transparency and honesty | 5 |
| LO6 | **Verify** information produced by AI before relying on it | 4, 5 |
| LO7 | **Use** AI responsibly for school work and problem-solving | 5 |

## How each topic is organised

Every topic file follows the same pattern, so learners always know where to look:

1. **Learning goals** and **key terms** at the top
2. Numbered sections (for example, *2.3 Major Stages of Development*), each with explanations, tables, diagrams and worked examples
3. **Core Insight** boxes that sum up each section
4. **Real cases** where they help (for example, *Mata v. Avianca* in Topic 04 and *Moffatt v. Air Canada* in Topic 05)
5. **Check your understanding** questions with answers, **links to other topics**, and **references** at the end

## Scope

**Included:** basic definitions, history and characteristics of AI; machine learning, deep learning and generative AI; how systems use data and algorithms; everyday and industry applications; benefits, limitations and bias; privacy, fairness, transparency and academic integrity; practical guidelines for checking AI output.

**Excluded:** programming tutorials; advanced mathematics of algorithms and neural networks; in-depth analysis of model architectures and training; detailed legal analysis of AI regulation across countries; extensive coverage of specialised fields such as advanced robotics or highly technical medical AI.

Simple explanations and relevant examples come first. Specific AI tools may appear as examples, but the module does not promote or review products.
