## Scope

**Included:** basic definitions, history and characteristics of AI; machine learning, deep learning and generative AI; how systems use data and algorithms; everyday and industry applications; benefits, limitations and bias; privacy, fairness, transparency and academic integrity; practical guidelines for checking AI output.

**Excluded:** programming tutorials; advanced mathematics of algorithms and neural networks; in-depth analysis of model architectures and training; detailed legal analysis of AI regulation across countries; extensive coverage of specialized fields such as advanced robotics or highly technical medical AI.

Simple explanations and relevant examples come first. Specific AI tools may appear as examples, but the module does not promote or review products.
