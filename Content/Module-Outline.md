# Module Outline: Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

**Module Title:** AI Overview: Understanding Artificial Intelligence  
**Target Learners:** University students and beginners with little or no prior AI knowledge  
**Total Estimated Study Time:** 4–5 hours (across all topics)  
**Module Status:** Stage 1 V1.0 — Topic 1 fully developed, Topics 2–5 in outline/draft stage

---

## 📋 Module Learning Goals

By the end of this module, learners will be able to:

1. **Explain** what artificial intelligence is and how it differs from traditional computer systems
2. **Describe** how AI systems work, including the roles of data, algorithms, and machine learning
3. **Identify** real-world applications of AI across different industries and domains
4. **Evaluate** the benefits, limitations, and challenges of AI systems
5. **Apply** ethical principles to the responsible use of AI in academic and professional contexts
6. **Critically assess** AI-generated information for accuracy, bias, and reliability

### Topic 4: Benefits, Limitations, and Challenges of AI

**Status:** 🟠 `[OUTLINE ONLY]`  
**Estimated Study Time:** 50–60 minutes  
**Prerequisites:** Completion of Topics 1–3

#### Learning Outcomes
By the end of this topic, learners will be able to:
1. Identify at least 3 major benefits of AI systems
2. Explain common limitations of AI, including hallucinations and lack of contextual understanding
3. Describe how data bias affects AI outputs and why it matters
4. Evaluate the risks of overdependence on AI and the importance of human oversight
5. Verify AI-generated information using fact-checking strategies

#### Planned Content
- Benefits of AI: efficiency, automation, productivity, 24/7 availability, handling large datasets
- Limitations: lack of true understanding, context sensitivity, common sense reasoning
- AI hallucinations: what they are, why they happen, examples
- Data quality and bias: how biased training data leads to biased outputs
- Overdependence on AI: when to trust AI, when to question it
- The importance of verification: fact-checking AI outputs, cross-referencing sources
- Case studies of AI failures and lessons learned

#### Planned Activities & Assessments
- **"Spot the Hallucination" Activity:** Given 3 AI-generated paragraphs, identify which contains fabricated facts
- **Bias Detection Exercise:** Analyze an AI chatbot's responses for potential bias
- **Verification Challenge:** Use an AI tool to generate information about a topic, then verify each claim using reliable sources
- **Risk Assessment:** For a given AI application, list 3 benefits, 3 limitations, and 3 risks

## 🔗 Cross-Topic Connections

This module is designed so that each topic builds on the previous one:

- **Topic 1 → Topic 2:** Understanding what AI is (Topic 1) provides the foundation for learning how different types of AI work (Topic 2)
- **Topic 2 → Topic 3:** Knowing how AI works (Topic 2) helps learners understand why it's applied in specific ways across industries (Topic 3)
- **Topic 3 → Topic 4:** Seeing AI applications (Topic 3) reveals both the benefits and limitations that need critical evaluation (Topic 4)
- **Topic 4 → Topic 5:** Understanding limitations and challenges (Topic 4) leads naturally to ethical considerations and responsible use (Topic 5)

## 📝 Scope Boundaries

### ✅ What This Module Covers
- Fundamental concepts of AI suitable for beginners
- Practical applications across multiple industries
- Critical evaluation of AI benefits and limitations
- Ethical principles and responsible use guidelines
- Development of AI literacy and critical thinking skills

### ❌ What This Module Does NOT Cover
- Programming tutorials or code implementation
- Advanced mathematical foundations of AI algorithms
- Technical details of neural network architectures
- In-depth legal analysis of AI regulations
- Specialized technical fields (advanced robotics, medical AI engineering)

**Rationale:** This module prioritizes conceptual understanding and practical literacy over technical implementation, making it accessible to learners from all academic backgrounds.
