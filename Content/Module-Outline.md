# Project Scope and Goals
### Target Learners
The target learners are university students and beginners who have little or no prior knowledge of Artificial Intelligence.  
The content is designed for learners who want to:
- Understand what AI is and how it differs from traditional computer systems.
- Learn how AI is used in everyday life, education, and the workplace.
- Recognize the benefits, limitations, and potential risks of AI.
- Develop the ability to evaluate AI-generated information critically and use AI responsibly.
### Learning Goals
By the end of this module, learners should be able to:
- Define Artificial Intelligence and explain its basic concepts in their own words.
- Describe how AI works at a general level, including the roles of data, algorithms, and machine learning.
- Identify common applications of AI in everyday life, education, business, and other fields.
- Explain the benefits and limitations of AI, including possible inaccurate outputs and bias.
- Recognize ethical concerns related to AI, such as privacy, fairness, transparency, and accountability.
- Evaluate AI-generated information critically and identify when information needs further verification.
- Apply responsible AI-use practices when completing academic tasks or solving everyday problems.

### Scope Boundaries
To keep the project focused and manageable, the group will cover the fundamental concepts of AI, its practical applications, and responsible use. The module will be introductory rather than highly technical.  

Materials included in the project:
- Basic definitions, history, and characteristics of AI.
- Different types of AI, including machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI.
- A general explanation of how AI systems use data and algorithms.
- Applications of AI in everyday life and various industries.
- Benefits of AI, such as efficiency, automation, and productivity.
- Limitations and challenges, including inaccurate outputs, bias, and overdependence.
- Ethical issues involving privacy, fairness, transparency, and academic integrity.
- Practical guidelines for evaluating AI-generated information and using AI responsibly.  

Materials excluded from the project:
- Detailed programming tutorials or instructions for building AI systems.
- Advanced mathematical explanations of AI algorithms and neural networks.
- In-depth technical analysis of AI model architectures and training processes.
- Detailed legal analysis of AI regulations across different countries.
- Extensive coverage of specialized fields, such as advanced robotics or highly technical medical AI.
- Speculative predictions about future AI developments beyond the module's learning objectives.  
Scope limitation: The project will use simple explanations and relevant examples to help beginners understand the subject. Examples of specific AI tools may be included to illustrate concepts, but the project will not focus on promoting or reviewing particular products.
### Questions for the Instructor
Before developing the learning materials, the group should confirm the following points with the instructor:
1. learners: Is the module intended for complete beginners, or are learners expected to have prior knowledge of AI?
2. Content outline: Are the five proposed topics sufficient to cover the required scope of an AI overview?
3. Technical depth: How detailed should the explanations of machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI be?
4. Examples and case studies: Can the group use current AI tools and real-world examples to explain the concepts?
5. Learning activities: Does each topic need exercises, quizzes, discussion questions, or practical activities?
6. Assessment criteria: What are the evaluation criteria for the completed learning module, and are there any specific formatting requirements?
